# Prompt Engineering Techniques

This document outlines various prompt engineering techniques and patterns that can be used to improve the performance of AI models. These techniques can be applied to various types of prompts, including persona prompts, instructional prompts, conversational prompts, and creative prompts.

## Prompt Patterns

What are prompt patterns?
Prompt patterns are structured approaches to designing prompts that can enhance the model's understanding and response quality. They help in creating effective prompts by leveraging specific techniques or strategies.

1. **Few-shot prompting**: Providing a few examples in the prompt to guide the model's responses.
2. **Chain-of-thought prompting**: Encouraging the model to think through a problem step-by-step.
3. **Contextual prompting**: Including relevant context or background information in the prompt to improve understanding.

## Prompt techniques

Prompt techniques are specific methods or strategies used to design prompts that elicit desired responses from AI models. These techniques can be applied across different types of prompts to enhance their effectiveness and the quality of the generated content.

1. **Persona-based prompting**: Designing prompts that reflect a specific character or personality to elicit responses that align with that persona.
2. **Instructional prompting**: Providing clear and specific instructions to guide the model's output.
3. **Conversational prompting**: Structuring prompts to simulate a dialogue or conversation, allowing for interactive responses.
4. **Creative prompting**: Encouraging the model to generate imaginative or artistic content by leaving room for interpretation and creativity.
5. **Iterative prompting**: Refining prompts based on previous outputs to improve the quality of responses.
6. **Role-based prompting**: Assigning a specific role or perspective to the model to shape its responses, such as asking it to act as an expert in a particular field.
7. **Conditional prompting**: Using conditional statements in prompts to guide the model's responses based on specific criteria or scenarios.
8. **Feedback-based prompting**: Incorporating feedback from previous interactions to refine and improve future prompts.
9. **Multi-turn prompting**: Structuring prompts to allow for multiple turns of interaction, enabling more complex dialogues and responses.
10. **Contextual embedding**: Embedding relevant context or background information within the prompt to enhance the model's understanding and response quality.
